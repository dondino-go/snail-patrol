# Playtest guide

The goal is to learn whether the core loop is fun for people who aren't us, and where it breaks. This is about the loop, not polish or art.

## Before you start

- **Getting the game to testers:** it's one file. Send `index.html` and have them open it in Chrome or Safari, or host it at a link (GitHub Pages works, like Bowl Bandit).
- **Keyboard only on a computer:** arrows or WASD to steer, **Space** to split, **Q** for Big Quack (once earned), **1–4** to pick upgrades, M to mute.
- Plan about 10 minutes: one full season is 5 days of at most 90 seconds, plus the night screens.

## How to run a session

1. **Say almost nothing.** *"It's a game about ducks on a wine farm. Play it however you like, and think out loud."* Don't explain patrols.
2. **Watch, don't help.** If they're stuck for over 30 seconds, it's fine to say "the title screen has the controls".
3. **Take notes on what they do, not what they say** (see the list below).
4. **Ask the questions at the end**, after the harvest screen.

## What to watch for

- [ ] Do they read the title screen, or skip it?
- [ ] How long until their **first split**? Do they split on purpose or by accident?
- [ ] Do they ever **rejoin** a patrol on purpose?
- [ ] Do they notice patrols stuck in puddles? Do they go and rescue them?
- [ ] Do they react to **"Row saved!"** or combos (a smile, a "ooh")?
- [ ] Do they look at the **rain** on the timer bar and race it?
- [ ] Which night upgrade do they pick first, and why (ask them to say it out loud)?
- [ ] Where do they get frustrated? What's the exact moment?
- [ ] Do they want to play **another season** without being asked?

## Questions after

1. In one sentence, what were you trying to do?
2. What was the most satisfying moment?
3. What annoyed you?
4. What did the patrols do? Did you trust them?
5. Would you play this again tomorrow? On your phone?
6. If you could change one thing, what would it be?

## What we most want to learn this round

- Do players **get** splitting without being told?
- Does the speed-vs-dumbness trade-off of patrols make sense to anyone but us?
- Is "clear it before the rain" the hook for other people too?
