## The one-liner

**You're the lead duck on a South African wine farm. The vines are crawling with snails. Clear them before the rain, without a drop of pesticide.**

A bite-size pixel art arcade game: Pac-Man meets Untitled Goose Game, with a little Stardew Valley.

---

## It's based on a true story

At **Vergenoegd Löw**, a wine estate near Stellenbosch in South Africa, about **1,000 Indian Runner ducks** go to work every morning. They march out to the vineyards and eat the snails, aphids and worms off the vines, instead of the farm using pesticides. The farm has done this for 40 years. The daily duck parade has become a tourist attraction, and neighbouring farms are starting to buy ducks of their own. *(RTÉ, August 2026)*

It's a lovely, slightly absurd and completely real story, and a great premise for a game.

---

## How it plays

- **You lead a line of ducks** around a vineyard block, Pac-Man style. Any duck that walks past a snail eats it.
- **Every block starts infested.** Clear every row of snails before they eat the grapes. Chain snails for combos, and clear a whole row for a satisfying **"Row saved!"**
- **Every duck behind you slows you down.** Press Space to send half the line off on patrol. Patrols are *gloriously dumb*: they mow the rows in a zig-zag and stop dead at the first puddle.
- **Rain is the deadline.** Every shower brings fresh snails onto rows you've already cleared. Can you clear the block before the next rain?
- **Five days, five different blocks**, with one upgrade each night (hatch a duckling, Big Quack, Brisk waddle…). Then **harvest**: your season becomes a wine label with a bottle count and a tasting note (*"Plum, pepper and a hint of pond."*).

## Where it is today

- A **playable prototype** that already works. It's one HTML file, keyboard controlled, with grey-box art.
- **Three versions in, the loop is found.** We tried a defend mode and a free-moving flock and parked both. The keeper is *restore the block before the rain*. Playtest quote: *"clearing rows feels great."*
- Balance has been tuned with bots, and real-player tests are under way.
- There's a backlog of ideas ready to pull from: aiming patrols, a farm-dog "ghost", tourists on the duck parade, named ducks.

**Come and play it before you decide:** https://dondino-go.github.io/snail-patrol/

## What we'd build at the hackathon

Take the prototype and make it *properly*: something you'd show your friends.

1. **Real pixel art**: ducks with a waddle, snails, vines, puddles, rain, and a lovely harvest label
2. **Sound and music**: quacks, a rising combo pitch, the "Row saved!" jingle, and a cheerful farm tune
3. **Better patrols**: aim when you split, and see where they'll go
4. **Hand-made maps** in a level editor (Tiled), so designers can make blocks without code
5. **A playable link plus a phone build** for the demo, so the audience can play on their own phones

**The demo:** live play on the big screen, with everyone in the room competing for the best harvest on their phones.
